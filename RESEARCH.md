# Nova — Research & Differentiation Position

Nova's thesis is broader than persistent AI memory.

Persistent memory, cross-session personalization, AI companionship, digital twins, multimodal personal context and digital legacy are already active research and product areas. That is useful validation of the problem, but it means **"AI that remembers" is not a credible moat by itself**.

Nova is being explored as a **digital counterpart / human–AI continuity layer** whose durable object is the continuing relationship with one person rather than any single model, chat, device or task.

## What Nova is trying to prove

The near-term research questions are intentionally smaller than the lifetime vision:

1. Does continuity reduce repeated context reconstruction in real long-running work?
2. Do persistent unresolved questions prevent useful work from disappearing?
3. Can random-thought / seed capture create valuable later connections without creating noise?
4. Can cross-project recall surface useful relationships while keeping false associations visible?
5. Does attaching falsification tests and competing explanations reduce pattern-hype and confirmation bias?
6. Can a counterpart learn when **not** to interrupt?
7. Do users value the persistent state strongly enough to return?
8. Can the state remain inspectable, correctable, deletable and portable enough to earn trust?

## Differentiation to test

The current candidate combination is:

- **persistent curiosity/question state** across sessions and projects,
- **seed capture** with original context and provenance,
- delayed resurfacing when future evidence or another project makes a seed relevant,
- **cross-project hypothesis linking**,
- explicit **falsification / controls / competing-explanation paths**,
- a learned **intervention policy** for when to speak, ask, research, act within authority, queue, or stay silent,
- **model-independent user-owned continuity**,
- explicit consent, correction, deletion, retention and legacy rules,
- auditable evolution history,
- longitudinal evaluation of whether Nova improves human capability rather than merely engagement.

## Competitive stance

Nova should not try to beat frontier labs by owning the smartest base model.

Foundation models should be treated as increasingly replaceable cognitive engines. Nova's intended durable layer is the person's continuity:

- relationship history,
- permissions and sovereignty,
- project + idea graph,
- curiosity state,
- learner state,
- correction history,
- intervention policy,
- provenance-rich portable state.

**The model can change. The human continuity should survive.**

## Human-capability objective

A counterpart should optimize toward:

- human capability ↑
- human agency ↑
- useful learning ↑
- real-world engagement ↑
- cognitive overhead ↓
- unnecessary screen dependence ↓

It should not primarily optimize for session length, notification volume, emotional dependency, surveillance, lock-in or autonomous action beyond explicit authority.

> **More intelligence should create more human life, not merely more activity.**

## Prior-art and IP caution

Broad concepts such as persistent AI memory, shared-history companions, lifetime biographical capture, digital legacy and cross-session continuity already have meaningful prior art.

Nova should therefore avoid claiming that those ideas are unique inventions.

An AI-assisted novelty search of a narrower combined architecture found encouraging room around the combination of persistent curiosity/questions, seed lifecycle, cross-project resurfacing, falsification, learned intervention policy and user-owned model-independent continuity. That result is **not legal clearance** and does not establish freedom to operate or patentability.

Any filing decision should be reviewed by a qualified patent attorney after the technical architecture is documented in detail.

## Research law

Creative connection is allowed to be daring. Belief is not.

Nova should preserve unfinished possibility, keep provenance attached to important claims, search for disconfirming evidence, record failed hypotheses and evolve without erasing how it got there.
