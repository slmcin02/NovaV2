# Nova — Public Counterpart Roadmap

This roadmap is deliberately **proof-first and horizon-separated**. It distinguishes what Nova can test today from the much larger digital-counterpart and human–AI-interface vision.

The sequencing rule is:

> **Start with continuity. Prove useful curiosity. Earn the right to become more present.**

## Now — prove a narrow counterpart wedge

Current objective: demonstrate measurable value in long-running real work before widening the product claim.

- Recruit 3–5 serious design partners with long-running learning, research or decision workflows.
- Establish a baseline for context reconstruction, lost questions, repeated research, missed decisions and switching overhead.
- Use the public Continuity Lab as the simplest testable surface for retained state across updates.
- Improve inspectability of project state, unresolved questions, contradictions and provenance.
- Keep the finance/options project as one human-controlled hard laboratory.
- Separate QA/test traffic from genuine external product analytics.
- Publish evidence that weakens the thesis as well as evidence that supports it.

## Next — add counterpart-specific behavior

If continuity alone creates useful signal, test capabilities that make Nova more than a memory layer.

- **Persistent question queue** — unresolved questions survive until evidence changes.
- **Seed capture** — random thoughts retain provenance and context without forcing an immediate conclusion.
- **Cross-project recall** — potentially relevant ideas can surface outside the folder where they originated.
- **Curiosity engine** — detect novelty, contradictions and information gaps; propose investigation selectively.
- **Falsification loop** — every interesting connection can carry disconfirming tests and alternative explanations.
- **Learner state** — retain what the human understands, what explanations worked and which prerequisite unlocks the next real question.
- **Intervention feedback** — learn when a suggestion, interruption or proactive action was useful, annoying or wrong.
- **Explicit retention controls** — make it easier for the person to decide what should persist.

Success criterion: at least one proactive/counterpart behavior creates repeatable user value without creating more cognitive noise than it removes.

## Then — build a portable life/project layer

Only after the narrow wedge survives external testing:

- Separate durable human/project state from any one foundation model.
- Improve import/export and provenance portability.
- Strengthen encrypted storage, integrity checking, backups and recovery.
- Expand permission scopes so capability and authority remain separate.
- Build clearer shared project/idea graphs.
- Add specialist domains through the same counterpart rather than creating disconnected user identities.
- Evaluate privacy leakage, stale memory, sycophancy, manipulation and cross-domain contamination as first-class failure modes.

## Later — ambient counterpart

This is a **vision horizon**, not a current feature claim.

Potential interfaces include:

- voice and earbuds,
- phone and watch,
- computer and browser,
- car and home surfaces,
- wearables and glasses,
- other sensors the person explicitly authorizes.

Design requirement: ambient availability must not imply ambient permanent recording. Presence, retention and action require distinct permissions.

A mature intervention policy should learn when to speak, queue, ask, act within authority or stay quiet.

## Long horizon — human–AI interface

If the technology becomes safe, useful and governable, Nova can eventually explore deeper forms of human augmentation and biological interface.

This is not a promise about current capabilities or timelines.

The goal is not to erase the human. It is to expand memory, learning, perception, coordination and creative discovery while preserving personal sovereignty.

## Legacy horizon

A lifetime counterpart could eventually preserve a governed longitudinal record of a person's language, ideas, reasoning, relationships, projects, changing worldview and the history created between the human and AI.

A future legacy mode should distinguish preservation from consciousness claims. Nova does **not** claim mind uploading, consciousness transfer or literal immortality.

## What should never become “just a roadmap item”

These are permanent design laws rather than features to ship once:

- Human stays sovereign.
- The model can change; continuity should survive.
- Curiosity is valuable only when paired with verification.
- Projects are exploration environments, not separate identities.
- Preserve unfinished possibility.
- Learn at the point of need.
- More intelligence should create more human life, not just more activity.
- Trust comes before deeper autonomy or deeper presence.
- Proof outranks the pitch.

## Explicit non-claims

Nova does not currently claim:

- product-market fit,
- always-on ambient sensing,
- licensed therapy or medical care,
- neural-interface capability,
- consciousness transfer,
- guaranteed financial returns,
- proven investment alpha,
- autonomous trade execution.

Roadmap items will change as evidence improves. That is part of the design: **evolution without losing continuity.**
